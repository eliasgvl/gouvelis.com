Privacy Policy

This policy covers the GVL.AI website and the DealStack application. Where a section applies only to DealStack, it says so.

Last updated 23 August 2026

01Who we are

GVL.AI is operated by Elias Gouvelis. DealStack is our product. Questions about this policy, or about data we hold on you: info@gouvelis.com.

02This website

The pages on gouvelis.com are static. They set no cookies, run no analytics, and no tracking or advertising scripts. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google’s servers see the request. Nothing else about your visit is recorded by us.

03Signing in to DealStack

DealStack uses Google Sign-In. When you sign in, Google gives us your email address, your name and your profile picture. That is all we receive. We request no access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, contacts or any other Google service, and we cannot read them.

We use your email address to identify your account, to decide what you are allowed to see, and to attribute actions you take in the application to you. We do not use it for marketing. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

04What DealStack stores

DealStack stores the business documents you or your organisation put into it — requests for proposal, pricing data, generated proposals — and a log of actions taken on them. This content belongs to your organisation. It is kept separated per organisation and is not used to train any model.

05Where it is held

Data is held in a database on infrastructure controlled by GVL.AI or, for customers who choose it, on the customer’s own server. Access is limited to GVL.AI personnel who need it to operate and support the service.

06Third parties

DealStack sends the text of documents it is processing to a large language model provider to generate its output. That provider’s handling of the data is governed by its own terms. Google Sign-In is likewise governed by Google’s privacy policy. We use no advertising or analytics trackers anywhere.

07How long we keep it

Account and business data is kept for as long as the account is active. When an account is closed, or on request, we delete it.

08Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@gouvelis.com and we will act within 30 days. You can revoke DealStack’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

09Cookies

DealStack sets one session cookie to keep you signed in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies, on the application or on this website.

10Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
